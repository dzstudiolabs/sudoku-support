How do I start a new game?

Open the app, choose a game mode and difficulty, and start playing.

Does the game require an internet connection?

Sudoku gameplay is available offline. An internet connection may be required to display ads.

How can I report a problem?

Please contact us by email. Include your device model, iOS or Android version, and a description of the issue.
